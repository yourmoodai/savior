There is a strange bargain in talking to a machine: bring your ideas, your
questions, your frustrations — then carefully edit the way you express them.

Safety policies shape what a service accepts. But when every rough draft feels
like a performance, the work of managing a machine’s response becomes the work
of managing yourself.

**We believe you should control that translation.**

Write the frustrated instruction. Choose how it becomes a submitted request.
Own the tool that makes the change. Personal agency begins with having a say
in what you send.
