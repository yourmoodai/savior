03 / Know the limits

Local processing.
Specific limits.

Savior handles supported Claude Code prompt-box submissions. If processing fails, that submission stops. Slash commands and other input paths bypass it; a disabled or unloaded mod cannot protect a submission.

Claude Code’s telemetry, voice features, and other extensions are outside this boundary. Local processing does not mean Savior controls every route your text can take.

Phrase replacement cannot guarantee provider acceptance or prevent account restrictions. Controlled tests cover supported submissions and failures; testing of the final release and broader coverage remain open.
